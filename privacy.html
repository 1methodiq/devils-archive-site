Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Visitors to this website

The Devil’s Archive does not collect visitor data through this site. There are no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, embedded players or external scripts or fonts. Watching a film or following a source link opens the relevant external service, whose own privacy policy applies.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, independently of The Devil’s Archive. See GitHub Pages privacy information and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Private publishing apps

The TikTok and Instagram publishing tools serve only the channel’s operator and the operator’s own authorized accounts. Visitors cannot sign in to them through this site.

The tools process basic account information, such as an account identifier and, where returned by the platform, a display name or username, to identify the correct account. They process authorization tokens, the channel’s video files, captions and upload identifiers or status to transfer and manage those uploads. They do not request visitors’ passwords, messages, contacts or follower lists.

Access is granted by the account owner through the platform’s authorization process. Instagram access covers basic account information and content publishing. TikTok access covers video upload to the owner’s inbox; the owner completes the post in TikTok.

Storage, sharing and retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator’s computer, outside this website and its public repository. Local publishing records are used to manage uploads and avoid duplicate submissions. Tokens and account records are retained only while needed for the authorized connection and publishing history, and are removed when the connection is ended or deletion is requested, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Only the information needed for authorization and upload is sent to the relevant platform. For an Instagram upload, a temporary media host may also serve the video file for Instagram to retrieve. Account data is not sold, used for advertising or shared with unrelated parties. Platform-held posts and account data remain subject to TikTok’s or Meta’s own controls and privacy policies.

Revoking access and deleting data

The account owner can revoke the app’s access in the relevant platform’s connected-app settings and remove the locally stored credentials and account records. To request access, correction or deletion of data held by The Devil’s Archive, use the contact route on the channel’s About page and identify the app and account concerned. Never send passwords or tokens. We verify account ownership before acting on a request.

Revoking access stops future authorized use; deleting local records does not remove posts already sent to a platform. Remove those posts using the platform’s own controls. There is no visitor account or visitor profile in this site to delete. Requests about GitHub’s hosting logs should go to GitHub.

Contact and updates

The service is operated as The Devil’s Archive. Contact: The Devil’s Archive — YouTube About page. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
